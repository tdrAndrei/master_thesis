\chapter{\label{chap:Preface}Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Preface}

It was hard to imagine this moment, where my thesis would be complete and it was time to write the final words. I have been studying at TU Delft for the last 5 years and it feels that only now I have a bit of a break to look back. Time feels different for all of us and while I was a student here, I felt it would last forever. Courses, assignment deadlines and studying with my friends made up almost all my time here, until it didn't. Starting this thesis meant I was in charge of my own schedule, holding myself accountable and unfortunately seeing my friends less and less, such is the proper adult life.

Firstly, I want to acknowledge and thank Jesper for his efforts. I have met him in my Bachelor's during the functional programming course, which amplified my passion for programming languages and made me want to study this further. I owe thanks to Jesper for his patience, guidance and the ability to see things from my perspective as well. It is hard to imagine doing the thesis with someone else.
Secondly, I would like to thank some people that were close to me during the writing of the thesis: Ana, my girlfriend, with whom I've shared this journey since the beginning and who wouldn't let me give up on making the thesis work and Matei for his advice and motivation. Also to all my friends and colleagues that I've met along the way who maybe weren't directly involved in the writing of this thesis, but with whom I shared experiences in these 5 years of studies, I want to thank you for being in my life and leaving a mark. I'm sure I will remember the fun we had over the years.

Now that I have to close this chapter of my life where I was a student, the future looks uncertain. It is clear to me that the world when I came in Delft 5 years ago is very different to the one we have now. I would like to continue in the world of computer science, but I am unlikely to follow academia. What I do know is that what I learned here in Delft will help me succeed wherever I may go.

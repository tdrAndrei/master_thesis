\section{\label{sec:related-work}Related work}

In this section, we compare our approach with existing work in the domain of dependent type elaboration, higher-order unification, and declarative name binding.

\subsection{Higher-order unification and elaboration}
In Miller's pattern fragment, metavariables are applied only to distinct bound
variables. Unification within this fragment is decidable and has most general
unifiers \autocite{miller_logic_1991}. Mazzoli
and Abel formulate dependent type checking as the generation and solution of
unification constraints \autocite{mazzoli2016typechecking}. The elaborator of
Lean uses unification to reconstruct implicit arguments and other omitted
information \autocite{demoura2015elaboration}, and Agda takes a similar approach
\autocite{norell2007practical}. Abel and Pientka go beyond the static fragment
with dynamic pattern unification, which postpones constraints outside the
fragment until other solutions bring them back into it, and with pruning,
which removes dependencies that would otherwise block a solution
\autocite{abel_extensions_2018}. Our Statix implementation solves only
constraints that are already patterns and implements neither postponement nor
pruning.

\subsection{Scope graphs and dependent typing in Statix}
Neron et al.\ introduced scope graphs as a language-independent model of name
resolution \autocite{neron_extensible_2015}. Statix combines scope graph
construction and queries with typing constraints in one declarative
specification language \autocite{van_antwerpen_scopes_2018,
4bf44aa1779c4a968c555e1b54e16119}. Brouwer specified a dependently typed
language in Statix, with type-level computation through substitutions stored in
the scope graph, but without higher-order unification
\autocite{none_dependently_2023}. Our language follows that design and adds
metavariables, a separate metavariable scope, and higher-order pattern
unification.

\subsection{Contextual metavariables}
Presentations of higher-order unification usually record the context of a
metavariable in the unification problem itself, for example as the contextual
type of a metavariable in contextual modal type theory
\autocite{nanevski2008contextual}. Our implementation does not store this
context separately. It recovers it by querying the scope graph for the binders
visible from the scope in which the metavariable was created.

\subsection{HOAS and logic programming}
In HOAS, object-language binders are represented by meta-language binders
\autocite{pfenning_higher-order_nodate}. Elpi is a $\lambda$Prolog dialect for
$\lambda$-tree syntax with built-in higher-order pattern unification and
suspension of constraints outside the fragment \autocite{dunchev_elpi_2015}. We
use it in \cref{sec:ScopeGraphs} as the point of comparison for our Statix
implementation.
